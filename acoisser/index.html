How it works

A digital archivist, available 24/7.

Every official document of the condominium — minutes, budget, contracts, quotes, insurance — is fed into the assistant's knowledge base. It never guesses: it retrieves, and cites.

🗂️

Everything read, nothing invented

Every answer cites its document and date. If the information isn't in the archives, the assistant says so — and points you to the right person.

🔒

Your documents stay private

The document base is hosted on your own infrastructure. Nothing is sent to third-party services, nothing is shared publicly, no training on your data.

🧾

Full traceability

Conflicts between documents are detected: the most recent version wins, and the assistant flags the discrepancy instead of hiding it.

Privacy

Designed for a condominium, not for Silicon Valley.

Your minutes contain names, amounts, sensitive decisions. ACOISSER was built on the opposite assumption from most AI tools: nothing should ever leave your building.

🏠

Sovereign hosting

The service runs on the condominium's private infrastructure. No account with a cloud giant, no data leaving Europe.

👥

One login per co-owner

Each co-owner gets personal credentials. Conversations are private: your questions are visible to no one else.

🚫

Zero training

Documents are used only to answer your questions. No model is trained on your data, no resale, no profiling.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can use the assistant? +
The co-owners of the condominium, each with their personal credentials. Access is granted by invitation.
Can the assistant be wrong? +
It can only answer from its documents, and always cites its source — you can check. If two documents contradict each other, it flags it. For legally binding questions, it will point you to the notary or the managing agent.
Are my questions visible to other co-owners? +
No. Each conversation is private. Only the document base is shared.
Which documents are included? +
General meeting minutes, deed and building rules, insurance policies, work quotes and invoices, supplier contracts, official contact list. Bank statements can be kept out of the base if the condominium prefers.
Does it replace the managing agent? +
No — by design. It's a smart archivist that saves you time. Decisions, signatures and legal obligations remain human.
