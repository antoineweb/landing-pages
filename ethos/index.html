06 / FAQ

Questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into.

What if I miss a day?

Days you didn't plan a habit never count against you. If you did it but forgot to log it, add it for yesterday in one tap, or fix any past day in the calendar.

Does it work without Apple Health?

Yes. Apple Health is optional. Without it, every habit can be logged by hand.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone only. ETHOS has no server. Deleting the app deletes your data.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with iOS 18 or later.

Why does it look like this?

Because a habit tracker is a tool. Monospace type, hard edges, no confetti. Just proof.
