Short videos that actually make you feel better. Goodwave surfaces uplifting content from channels you love, tuned to the topics you pick, with a distraction-free full-screen player.

  • Positive-only feed: every video passes a positivity scoring filter, so negativity never hits your screen.
  • Your topics: choose what you care about and let the feed refresh itself daily with fresh picks.
  • Distraction-free player: full-screen vertical feed, tap to pause, swipe for the next video, no algorithmic rabbit holes.

  • Privacy by design: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your preferences stay on your device.
