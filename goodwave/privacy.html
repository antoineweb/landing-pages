Goodwave Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Goodwave collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

Video content

Goodwave plays videos hosted by their creators on YouTube. The app refreshes its catalog daily using the YouTube Data API. These requests contain only public content queries and are not linked to any identity.

Preferences

Your topic choices and watch signals (skips, likes, completions) are stored on your device with UserDefaults and never sent anywhere. Watch signals never leave your device.

Permissions

Goodwave requests no sensitive permissions. No location, no contacts, no health data.

Children

Goodwave is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
