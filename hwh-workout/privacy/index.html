Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

  • HWH Workout collects no data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no trackers.
  • Your workouts stay on your iPhone, and the app works without an internet connection.
  • You can export your history or delete all of it from the app at any time.

1. Who we are

HWH Workout is an iPhone app published by Antoine Schuppisser (Rook Labs). Contact: artusbe@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • Your workouts: dates, durations, exercises and reps, plus the workouts you create or edit.
  • Your settings: accent colour, voice coach and music choices.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We can't see it. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or on a computer, iOS may include this data in your own backup.

3. Your music library

If you turn on Music in the app, iOS asks for your permission to access your music library, so you can pick a playlist to play during workouts. The list of playlists is only read on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. You can turn this off at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music.

4. The voice coach

The coach's voice is produced on your iPhone by iOS. The app doesn't use your microphone and doesn't record you.

5. Exporting and importing

You can export your history as a CSV file from Settings › Your Data. The file only goes where you choose to send it. Importing reads a file you choose, on your iPhone.

6. What we share

Nothing. We don't collect your data, so we don't sell it or share it. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

7. This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to run the service.

8. Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings › Your Data › Reset All Data, or delete the app.
  • Questions: write to artusbe@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. Children

HWH Workout is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the app before it does.

Support

A real person answers.

Write to artusbe@gmail.com and tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

  • The coach is silent: check that your iPhone isn't muted and that the voice is on in Settings › Voice Coach.
  • A better voice: download a Premium English voice in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English. It then appears in Settings › Voice Coach.
  • Moving to a new iPhone: export your history as a CSV from Settings › Your Data, then import it on the new iPhone.
  • A preset is gone: Settings › Your Data brings back Express, Steady, Burn and All Out.

HWH Workout gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts.
