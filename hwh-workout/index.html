11 / FAQ

Questions.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every move uses your body weight. Tricep dips want a chair or a step and wall sits want a wall; if you don't have them, take those moves out of your workout.

I haven't trained in years. Is it for me?

Yes. Start with Express: 6 minutes on Easy, with lower rep targets. Every move is shown and explained, and you can pause or skip one anytime. When it feels easy, try Steady, or raise the difficulty.

How long is each move?

15 seconds of work after a 3-second preview. Ready sooner? Tap “Start now”. Want to skip one? There's a button for that too.

Can I listen to my own music?

Yes. Start Spotify, Apple Music or a podcast before the workout and it keeps playing, lowered while the coach speaks. Or let the app play a playlist from your music library.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Everything runs and stays on your iPhone.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with iOS 17 or later.
