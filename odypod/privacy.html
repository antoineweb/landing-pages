PRIVACY POLICY

Your files. Your data.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Short version

Odypod collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It plays your own Music library and files. There is nothing to sell, leak or subpoena — because there is no data.

What the app does

Odypod is a private, local music and audiobook player. It plays the songs in your Music library, or audio files from the folder you choose (for example "Odypod Media" in your iCloud Drive) or from On My iPhone › Odypod. Playback position is saved on your device. That's the entire app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no tracking, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and makes no network requests except reading your own iCloud Drive. It has no user accounts. We — the developer — cannot see, access, or receive any of your data.

iCloud Drive

Your media stays in your own iCloud Drive, under Apple's privacy policy and encryption. Odypod reads the folder you point it at and never uploads, copies, or transmits your files anywhere else.

Permissions

  • Media & Apple Music (optional). Odypod asks to read your Music library so it can play your songs, playlists and albums. It reads them on your device only and never sends anything anywhere. If you decline, Odypod plays your files instead.
  • Nothing else. No contacts, no location, no photo library, no microphone. Besides your Music library, it only touches the files and folder you choose.

Children

Odypod is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data, no data of children is collected either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
