WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Own your library again

The wheel

Glide your finger in an arc to scroll, press the center to select, BACK to go up a level. Every notch is a haptic tick, with an optional click sound. On Now Playing, the wheel sets the volume.

Folders, not databases

Play your Music library, or your own folders: On My iPhone or iCloud Drive, with Music and Audiobooks inside. Sort with 001_ prefixes, the app plays them in order. What you see in Files is what you hear in Odypod.

Zero friction

No sign-in, no uploads, no algorithm. The app has no servers of its own. Your position is remembered for every file. Nothing to configure.
