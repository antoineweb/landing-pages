AirPace Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

AirPace collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

Air quality data

AirPace fetches public air quality readings (AQI, PM2.5) for the cities you add. These requests contain city coordinates only and are not linked to any identity. Apple's built-in weather data may be used as a fallback source.

Health data

AirPace reads your steps, walking, running, cycling and workout data from Apple Health (HealthKit) to show your activity. This data is read-only, stored on your device and never sent anywhere. HealthKit data never leaves your device.

Location

Your location is used only to show air quality for where you are and to find nearby training spots. Location is not stored, not linked to any identity and not shared with anyone.

Permissions

  • Health: read your activity to display it in the app. Read-only.
  • Location (when in use): air quality for your current city and nearby spots.

Children

AirPace is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
