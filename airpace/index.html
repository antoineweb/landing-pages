AirPace Clean Air Training

Air quality aware trainingHealthKit readyiPhone + Apple Watch

Train at the right time, in the right air. AirPace combines live air quality (AQI, PM2.5) with your HealthKit activity to tell you when running, cycling or walking is actually good for you today.

  • Air quality first: every city on your list gets an instant AQI reading with clear guidance: go now, wait, or find a better moment.
  • Multi-city: up to 10 cities, so you know the air before you travel.
  • Activity, honestly: steps, walking, running, cycling and workouts from HealthKit, read-only, never leaving your device.
  • Spots nearby: parks, tracks and stadiums around you, with air quality for each.
  • Privacy by design: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Health data never leaves the device.
