WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Stop living in browser tabs

Every account, one list

Gmail, iCloud and any IMAP server, side by side in a unified inbox. Read, reply, mark as read — from the account that owns the thread.

Skin-first architecture

The interface is decoupled from the engine. Switch between "Chromium" (modern, clean) and "RetroMail 99" (Outlook Express-era nostalgia) in settings. More skins to come.

Native, not Electron

SwiftUI from scratch. IMAP/SMTP spoken natively, no heavyweight web-wrapper. Fast, small, and it looks like a Mac app should.

Organized by sender

Emails from the same sender across accounts are grouped together, so the threads that matter surface instead of drowning.
