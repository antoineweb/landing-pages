PRIVACY POLICY

Your email. Your device.

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Applies to the app "Every inbox" (formerly Unimail) for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Short version

Every inbox is a client for your own email accounts. Your mail goes directly between your device and your mail provider (Gmail, iCloud, IMAP). There is no Every inbox account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Two optional features send limited data elsewhere, explained below: AI sorting (only if you allow it) and company logos (can be turned off).

How it works

Every inbox connects to the email accounts you add using the standard IMAP and SMTP protocols, or Google's Gmail API if you sign in with Google. Mail is fetched to your device, displayed there, and sent through your provider. Your passwords and sign-in tokens are stored in your device's Keychain and are only ever sent to your mail provider.

AI sorting (optional, asked first)

To decide which emails are important, Every inbox first uses your own rules on your device: your VIP list (whitelist), muted senders, starred mail and Gmail's Promotions / Social categories. Those emails are never sent anywhere.

For the other emails, and only after you tap "Allow AI sorting", the app sends, over an encrypted connection: the sender's name and address, the subject, Gmail labels and the first 300 characters of the email. Depending on the engine you choose in Settings › AI filter:

  • Unimail AI (default): the data goes to our relay, hosted on Supabase, which forwards it to Ollama Cloud, an AI provider, and returns only "important / not important" and a short reason. The relay does not store email content. To prevent abuse it counts requests per random install identifier and per IP address; these counters are deleted after about two days.
  • Claude (your own key): the data goes directly to Anthropic under your own agreement with Anthropic.
  • On-device: nothing leaves your device.

If you say no, or turn it off later in Settings › AI filter, nothing is sent and local rules sort your mail. The AI never receives attachments, full message bodies, your passwords or your contacts list.

Whitelist suggestions

When you ask the app to suggest your important senders, it reads your Sent and starred mail on your device. If AI sorting is allowed, it may also send the candidate addresses, how often you exchanged emails with them and up to three subjects per sender, so the AI can pick and categorise them. Nothing is sent if AI sorting is off.

Company logos (optional)

To show a company's logo next to its emails, the app requests the logo of the sender's web domain (for example "example.com", never the full address) from Google's public favicon service. Personal domains such as gmail.com or icloud.com are never looked up. You can turn this off in Settings › Show company logos.

What we collect

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no sale or sharing of data for advertising. The developer cannot read your mailbox. Apart from the AI sorting and logos described above, no data leaves your device except to your mail provider.

Sample inbox

The "Explore a sample inbox" mode shows made-up emails only. It connects to nothing and sends nothing.

Your mail providers

Gmail, iCloud and other providers process your email under their own privacy policies. Every inbox requests only the access needed to read, organise and send mail on your behalf.

Children

Every inbox is not directed at children under 13.
