PRIVACY POLICY

Your email. Your device.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Short version

Unimail is a client for your own email accounts. It talks directly to your mail servers (Gmail, iCloud, IMAP) — there is no Unimail server, no Unimail account, and no Unimail analytics. Your credentials and mail data never touch anything but your mail provider.

How it works

Unimail connects to the email accounts you add, using the standard IMAP and SMTP protocols and your provider's official authentication. Mail is fetched to your device, displayed, and sent through your provider's SMTP server. Nothing in between.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer operates no servers, keeps no logs of your usage, and embeds no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Account credentials are stored in your device's Keychain, encrypted by macOS. The developer cannot see, access, or receive any of your email.

Your mail providers

Gmail, iCloud and other providers each process your email under their own privacy policies. Unimail requests only the access needed to read and send mail on your behalf.

Keychain

Account credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain, protected by your login password. They are never exported or transmitted anywhere except to your mail provider for authentication.

Children

Unimail is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data, no data of children is collected either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
