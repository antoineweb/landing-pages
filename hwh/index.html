07 / FAQ

Questions.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone counts your steps on its own. Sleep phases, heart rate variability and resting heart rate come from an Apple Watch, or from any app or device that writes them to Apple Health.

Does it work without Apple Health?

Yes. Every data type can be added and edited by hand, on the iPhone or on the web.

What if a day looks wrong?

Edit it, or leave it out of your averages without deleting it. A day you corrected is never overwritten by the next import, and a day you deleted stays deleted.

Do I need the web app?

No, but you get it with the same account. The iPhone shows your scores, charts and knight; the web app adds every detail. Both always show the same data.

Where is my data, and can I delete it?

In your HWH account, which only you can read. Delete your account in the app's settings and every entry goes with it. Your Apple Health data itself stays in Apple Health.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with iOS 17 or later. The web app works in any browser.
