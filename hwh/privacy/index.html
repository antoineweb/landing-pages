Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short

  • HWH collects only what it needs to show your Health, Wealth and Happiness scores: your account, the entries you add, and the Apple Health data you choose to import.
  • Your health data, including Apple Health data, is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The iPhone app contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • You can delete your account, and every entry with it, from the app or the web at any time.

1. Who we are

HWH is an iPhone app and a web app (hwh-nine.vercel.app) that share one account. HWH is published by Antoine Schuppisser (Rook Labs), who is responsible for your data. Contact: artusbe@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and name, and a password if you create one. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, the identifier and email address they share with us.
  • Your date of birth, only if you enter it on the web.

What you enter

  • Wealth: assets, debts, income, expenses and net worth snapshots.
  • Happiness: mood scores and notes, social quality, places you stayed, relationship groups and creative sessions.
  • Health entries you type yourself, and your avatar (name and level).

Apple Health (iPhone app, only with your permission)

If you allow it, the iPhone app reads: sleep phases, steps, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, body fat percentage, mindful minutes, cycling distance and the duration of cycling workouts. You choose which types to allow in the system permission sheet, and how much history to import.

Subscriptions (web only)

Paid plans are sold on the web through Stripe. We receive your plan, its status and its dates. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us. The iPhone app sells nothing.

On your iPhone

The app keeps a local copy of your entries, so it works offline, and a few settings (such as the period you chose and how far the Apple Health import has gone). Your session is kept in the iOS Keychain.

3. How we use it

Only to provide HWH: to compute and show your scores, averages, charts and avatar, and to keep your iPhone and the web app in sync. We don't use your data for profiling, and we never make automated decisions about you.

4. Apple Health data

  • Apple Health data is used only to provide HWH's health features: your Health score, averages and charts.
  • It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with advertising platforms or data brokers, and never used to build profiles.
  • It is stored in your HWH account (see section 6), so the web app can show it. It is not stored in iCloud.
  • HWH only reads Apple Health. It never writes to it.
  • You can stop it at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › HWH, delete imported days in the app, or delete your account.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use a few providers that process it on our behalf, only to run HWH:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Your account and entries are stored there, in the United States (US East).
  • Vercel: hosting of the web app.
  • Stripe: web payments, if you subscribe.
  • Apple and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them.

On the website only, we may send limited events, such as a sign-up or a purchase, to Meta to measure our advertising. These events never include health data, Apple Health data or the content of your entries. The iPhone app sends nothing to Meta or any other advertising service.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored with Supabase in the United States. Access rules on the database let only your own account read or change your entries. By using HWH from outside the United States, you understand that your data is transferred there.

7. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your login and every entry are deleted, and a live subscription is cancelled. Deleting an entry removes its content right away; a marker without content is kept so your other devices know it was deleted.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the iPhone app, ☰ › Settings › Account › Delete account, or on the web account page.
  • Edit or delete entries: in the app or on the web, at any time.
  • Apple Health: allow or revoke each type in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › HWH.
  • Access, correction, export, objection: write to artusbe@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. If you live in the EU, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

HWH is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. For important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email.
